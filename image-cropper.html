How to crop an image online

  1. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image from your device.
  2. Enter the left and top positions for the crop area in pixels.
  3. Enter the crop width and height.
  4. Select Crop image to preview the result.
  5. Select the download button to save the cropped image.

What do the crop position and dimensions mean?

The left position is the number of pixels from the image's left edge. The top position is the number of pixels from its top edge. Crop width and crop height determine the size of the selected area. For example, a left position of 0 and a top position of 0 starts the crop at the image's top-left corner.

Why crop an image?

Cropping removes unwanted outer areas of a picture and helps focus attention on the part you want to keep. It can be useful when preparing images for documents, presentations, websites or social posts. Cropping changes the visible area of an image; it does not simply change the image's display size.

Frequently asked questions

Is this image cropper free?

Yes. ToolNova's image cropper is free to use in your browser.

Which image formats can I use?

You can upload JPG, PNG and WebP images. The cropped image is downloaded as a PNG file.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. The image is processed locally in your browser and is not sent to a ToolNova server.

What happens if my crop area is outside the image?

The tool checks the crop position and dimensions. If the selected area extends beyond the original image, it asks you to correct the values.

Does cropping resize the whole image?

No. Cropping selects and keeps a rectangular part of the original image. Use the Image Resizer when you want to change the dimensions of the full image.
